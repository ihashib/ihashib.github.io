Sensometer

AquaLink · 2021 to 2022

Industrial sensor-to-cloud monitoring and control device. I built and maintained its firmware: an STM32F4 main MCU with an ESP32 co-processor, reading sensors over RS485/MODBUS and I2C, switching connected equipment, and handling real-time telemetry and secure data pipelines.

  • Embedded C
  • C++
  • STM32F4
  • ESP32
  • MODBUS/RS485
  • I2C

Hydrometer

AquaLink · 2021 to 2022

Field device for aquaculture and agriculture. Firmware for an STM32F2 with an ESP8266 co-processor, dual RS485/MODBUS sensors and low-power operation, plus the driver and GUI for its ST7567 display.

  • Embedded C
  • STM32F2
  • ESP8266
  • MODBUS/RS485

Greenhouse monitoring and farming robot

Undergraduate thesis · BRAC University

My undergraduate thesis: a greenhouse system that monitors growing conditions, uses a farming robot to grow and tend tomatoes, and predicts when the crop will be ready to harvest and when the greenhouse needs maintenance.
