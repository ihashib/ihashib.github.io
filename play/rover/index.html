Rover Run

Bring back three samples

Drive to the three sample sites and return to base. Stay inside radio range: drop relays to reach further, or the link goes fuzzy and the rover crawls. Rocks cost time.

  • ←↑↓→ or WASD to drive, Space to drop a relay
  • Use the joystick below to drive, and the Relay button to drop relays

Samples delivered

Drive to the three samples, then back to base. Drop relays to stay in radio range. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.
