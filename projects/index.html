Clear Frame

2023

Batch image cleaner for e-commerce product photos, built for the graphics team at Daraz. Detects people with MobileNetV3, cuts out the subject with U-2-Net, then cleans, heals or replaces the background without touching subject detail.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Tkinter
  • MobileNetV3
